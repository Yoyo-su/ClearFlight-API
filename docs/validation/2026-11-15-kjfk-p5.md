## Why it can't be assessed
1. ~~**No arrivals board for the hour.**~~ (Withdrawn; see the correction above.) The departures list loaded completely (12:00–18:00 local, 865 rows). The arrivals list stopped loading at 16:05 local after two attempts, and the evening window started at 18:45. I stopped there rather than work around the page.
2. **The recording was too short for a gate-time board.** Departures that pushed back after about 22:40 UTC took off after the recording ended (23:10), and §9 allows a departure up to 45 min after off-block. For gate-time boards, recordings must run to the end of the hour plus 45 min.
3. **An identity gap: US regional operators.** Endeavor (`EDV`) and Republic (`RPA`) fly the marketing carrier's flight number under their own prefix: `EDV5150` is board flight DL5150, `RPA4501` is AA4501. Neither identity rule covers it, and the board gives no aircraft type, so the second rule can't apply.

## Diagnostic only (departures, not the gate)
Departures alone under the fixed rule: 15 of 37 board departures found, 9 of 29 identified detections unmatched. Of the 22 missed departures, most pushed back after 22:40 (reason 2); several unmatched detections are regional-operator callsigns (reason 3).

## What a valid JFK hour needs (proposal, for an owner decision)
- record to the end of the hour plus 45 min;
- the arrivals capture as done (time-window filter, enough "Load more" clicks; checked by counting rows per scheduled hour, not by first and last rows);
- a regional-operator rule, fixed before recording: a callsign whose prefix is a listed regional operator (`EDV`, `RPA`, `SKW`, `ENY`, `JIA`, `ASH`, `GJS`, `PDT`) matches a board flight of any partner carrier with the same flight number.
