## What this shows
- **A detection gap, not just a validation gap.** At Kansai, receivers lose aircraft low over the runway, as at Incheon (the RKSI fixture lost 5 of 12 movements to one-poll dropouts). An aircraft last seen on the ground that reappears climbing a poll or more later fits neither path: the observed path needs consecutive polls, and the inferred path needs the aircraft to be first seen airborne. That is a design question for the owner.
- Provider timeouts again ended watched windows (2 of 7 misses).
- The comparison counts cargo and charter traffic as false because passenger boards don't list it.
