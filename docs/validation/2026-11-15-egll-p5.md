# P5 validation: EGLL (London Heathrow), 2026-11-15 07:00–08:00 UTC

**Result: not validated** (found 42.5%, false 54.9%; P5 needs ≥90% found and ≤5% false). Diagnostics show the main cause is the identity rule: ADS-B callsigns at Heathrow are mostly ATC alphanumeric callsigns that can't be tied to board flight numbers. That needs an owner decision on the protocol (below). The airport and both methods stay `experimental`.

## Recording
- adsb.lol `/v2/point` 25 nm, every 30 s, 06:55–08:10 UTC, exactly as the app polls: 148 usable polls, no failed polls, one watched window. Replayed through the committed detector (`scripts/replay_movements.py`, branch `feat/cf-011-observed-movements` at `cecd145`).
- Coverage mode: `surface`. Detections 06:55–08:10: 51 observed arrivals, 39 observed departures, no inferred movements. Raw polls are kept locally only (`.local-smoke/p5/`, not committed).

## Ground truth
- Heathrow's public departures and arrivals boards, loaded once each in a headless browser at 08:15 UTC (owner choice V5). The board's own data feed lists operating flights only (`excludeCodeShares=true`) with an ICAO flight identifier, scheduled times and status text.
- **Actual times** come from the status: "Landed HH:MM" (runway, landed) and "Departed HH:MM". London time equals UTC on this date. Cancelled flights are left out.
- **"Departed" doesn't state off-block or airborne.** Fixed before the comparison: it matches within either §9 departure tolerance (−5 to +45 min).
- **Identity:** the board gives each operating flight's ICAO identifier (e.g. `TAP1363`), so matching is direct on ICAO prefix + number, stricter than the §9 mapping table and equivalent to it.

## Diagnostics (post hoc, not the gate)
- **Counts in the hour:** departures detected 29 vs board 28; arrivals detected 42 vs board 45. Aggregate detection looks close to complete, but counts can't show which movements are false or missed.
- **Relaxed identity** (ignore a trailing letter suffix, e.g. `BAW564Y` → BA564): found 53.4%, false 43.7%. Most British Airways and Shuttle callsigns (`BAW35LF`, `SHT6Q`) aren't derived from the flight number at all, so suffix stripping doesn't fix identity.
